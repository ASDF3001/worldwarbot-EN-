# --- World War Bot Tips ---
Tips: Large empires with 3+ territories enjoy a 10% discount on defense reinforcements.
Tips: When choosing attack targets, you can type either ISO codes (e.g. JPN, USA) or full country names.
Tips: You can toggle the attack confirmation dialog on/off anytime using /user_setting.
Tips: Daily payouts occur every 12 hours (07:00 & 19:00 UTC). Manage your maintenance costs wisely!
Tips: If your treasury drops below 1,000 Gold, you can issue War Bonds with /war_bonds to borrow 5,000 Gold.
Tips: Joining the United Nations (UN) grants bonus Gold and Oil whenever you work (/work).
Tips: The expedition penalty reduces your effective attack power when attacking non-adjacent territories.
Tips: Funds invested during an invasion not only bypass penalties but also determine your initial defense upon capture.
Tips: If you run out of Oil, territory maintenance becomes impossible. Always maintain a strategic reserve!
Tips: Use /defend to bulk reinforce multiple territories simultaneously for efficient defense management.
Tips: Typing "ALL" in /defend divides your defense budget evenly across all your territories.
Tips: Surprise attacks (without formal declaration) calculate enemy defense as 1.5x, making them riskier.
Tips: Keep DM notifications enabled to receive instant alerts when your empire or allies are attacked.
Tips: If participating in multiple Worlds, switch your active world easily using channel commands.
Tips: Use /import_oil to propose direct resource trades with other players.
Tips: War Bond debts are automatically deducted from your daily payouts until fully repaid.
Tips: Battles include randomized combat factors; always attack with a comfortable budget buffer.
Tips: Form diplomatic Camps (/camp_create) with allies to coordinate coordinated defense and operations.
Tips: When in doubt, open the interactive GUI dashboard anytime using /command.
Tips: Fair play and diplomacy make the war exciting. Enjoy the conquest!
