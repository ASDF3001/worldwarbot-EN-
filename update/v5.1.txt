[World War Bot v5.1]

Major expansion of domestic policy, tech research, and espionage systems!
Master new commands and interactive UI to achieve global supremacy!

━━━ New Features & Commands ━━━

【Domestic】/invest (Infrastructure Investment)
- Invest Gold into national infrastructure.
- Total investments accumulate in your national status profile.

【Tech】/research (Tech Research)
- Spend Gold and Oil to increase your "Tech Level".
- Higher Tech Levels boost the success rates of espionage and sabotage operations!

【Espionage】/spy (Infiltration)
- Send spies to target nations to reveal confidential info: defense forces, Gold, and Oil reserves.
- Higher Tech Levels significantly improve infiltration success.

【Sabotage】/propaganda (Propaganda Campaign)
- Incite civil riots in enemy territory to slash their defense forces by 10% to 20%!
- Ideal for weakening targets prior to an invasion.

【Stats】/stats (Interactive Dashboard)
- Introduced tabbed UI for switching between "Economy", "Military", and "Trophies"!
- View your nation's status at a single glance.

【Ranking】/ranking (Leaderboards)
- Real-time top 10 leaderboards for Treasury, Territories, and Defense!

【Trophies】/trophy (Achievements & Titles)
- Check win rates, unlocked trophies, and currently equipped titles.

【GUI】/command (Easy GUI Panel)
- Added new options (Invest, Research, Spy, Sabotage) to the dropdown menu!

━━━ System Improvements ━━━
- Modularized codebase into Cog structure for improved stability and processing speed.
- Extended database schema to support tech levels and achievement records.
